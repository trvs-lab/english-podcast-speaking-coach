先进入一个真实情境：你在公司会议里看一份损益表，发现差旅和娱乐开支很不对劲。这节课我们会练 10 个左右的核心表达：怎么说“审阅报表”“费用飙高”“数字离谱”“失控”“我会调查/处理”等。练习时一时说不出来也没关系；如果卡住，直接回复「给我提示」。

Board member: We’re ready for the financial update.

你是会议主持人。你想说：现在进入议程最后一点，请 Jill 一起仔细看 profit and loss statement（损益表）。请用英文直接说给 Jill。

> let's go the last point of our agenda. Jill, let's go over the profit and loss statement.

这句会议感已经出来了，尤其是 `go over the profit and loss statement` 用得对。

更自然可以这样说：

```text
Let's move on to the last point on our agenda. Jill, let's go over the profit and loss statement.
```

这里主要改两处：
- `go the last point` 少了方向感，常说 `move on to...` 或 `go to...`
- `on our agenda` 是固定搭配，表示“在议程上”，不是 `of our agenda`

Jill: Sure. I’ve reviewed the numbers.

你现在换成 Jill。你想说：主要问题是，公司定期花的钱高得离谱。请用英文说。

> the main issue is our expenditures are through the roof.

这句能听懂，而且 `through the roof` 用得很准。这里更贴近这课的说法是：

```text
The main issue is that our expenses are through the roof.
```

小区别：
- `expenses` = 公司经常要花的钱，比如差旅、房租、工资、电话费
- `expenditures` = 某一类或某个项目上的总支出，更像财务报告里的正式词

Manager: I’m looking at the numbers now.

你现在是经理。你想说：这些数字高得离谱，这是怎么回事？请用英文说。

> these numbers are off the charts, what's going on here?

漂亮，这句可以直接放进会议里说：

```text
These numbers are off the charts. What's going on here?
```

注意书面里两个完整句子别只用逗号连起来；用句号会更稳。口语里停顿一下就很自然。

Jill: I found several unusual bills.

你继续当 Jill。你想说：公司在 entertainment（招待/娱乐）和 travel（差旅）上的总支出已经失控了。请用英文说。

> our expenditures on entertainment and travel are out of control.

这句很稳，`expenditures on...` + `are out of control` 搭得很好。可以直接说：

```text
Our expenditures on entertainment and travel are out of control.
```

如果更正式一点，也可以说 `the company’s expenditures...`，但你这句已经自然。

Manager: Can you give me an example?

你继续当 Jill。你想让经理看这些账单，并说明：光这个月，公司就为 hotel charges（酒店费用）付了超过 $20,000。请用英文说。

> This month, for example, we paid over $20000 for hotel charges.

这句自然，信息也完整。会议里可以稍微更像财务汇报一点：

```text
Just this month, we've paid over $20,000 for hotel charges.
```

小点：
- `Just this month` 强调“光这个月就……”
- `we've paid` 更像“到目前为止已经付了”
- 金额书写习惯是 `$20,000`

Manager: Hmm. I see.

你现在是经理。你想说：好的，谢谢，我会调查一下。请用英文说。

> All right, thank you, I'll look into it.

这句很好，`I'll look into it` 正是这里要的“我会调查一下”。写出来时把完整句子分开会更清楚：

```text
All right, thank you. I'll look into it.
```

Jill: There’s more.

你继续当 Jill。你想说：问题还没完；这里还有一张 $5,000 的 spa treatments（水疗护理）账单。请用英文说。

> Here's a $50,000 bill for spa treatments.

句子结构很自然，不过金额和原场景不一样：这里是 `$5,000`，不是 `$50,000`。如果你想按这课情境说，可以这样：

```text
Here's a $5,000 bill for spa treatments.
```

如果要带出“问题还没完”的感觉，可以加一句：

```text
The list goes on and on. Here's a $5,000 bill for spa treatments.
```

Manager: That’s enough detail for now.

你现在是经理。你想说：谢谢，到这里就可以了；我会处理。请用英文说。

> Thank you, that'll be all. I'll take care of it.

这句可以用，而且语气很像经理在“赶紧结束这个尴尬话题”。

更干净一点：

```text
Thank you. That'll be all. I'll take care of it.
```

`I'll look into it` 偏“我去查一下”；`I'll take care of it` 更像“我会处理掉/负责解决”。

Jill: Wait, there’s one more bill.

你继续当 Jill。你想说：看这一张，光一晚就在一个叫 Wild Things 的地方报销了 $8,000。请用英文说。

> look at this, they spend $8,000 at a place called Wild Things.

这里要修一下时态和“报销/记账”的说法。自然版本：

```text
Look at this one. $8,000 was expensed in one night at a place called Wild Things.
```

这里主要改三处：
- `spend` 要用过去式：`spent`，因为是在讲已经发生的账单。
- 这课里更财务化的说法是 `was expensed`，意思是“被报销/记作费用”。
- 你漏了 `in one night`，它强调“就一个晚上”。

先不照抄上面的句子。换个很近的场景：你发现同事光一个下午就在一个叫 Blue Moon 的地方报销了 `$3,000`。请用英文说。

> $3,000 was spent in one afternoon at a place called Blue Moon by my colleague.

这句语法上能成立，但信息顺序有点笨重，`by my colleague` 放最后会显得像硬翻译。更自然：

```text
My colleague expensed $3,000 in one afternoon at a place called Blue Moon.
```

或者如果你想强调“这笔钱被报销了”：

```text
$3,000 was expensed in one afternoon at a place called Blue Moon.
```

小点：
- 人做主语时：`My colleague expensed $3,000...`
- 钱做主语时：`$3,000 was expensed...`
- `spent` 只是“花了”，`expensed` 更像“报销/入账”，更贴合这课。

Manager: Okay, okay, I understand.

你现在是经理，想有点尴尬地收住话题：我明白了。谢谢你非常 thorough（细致、全面）的分析。请用英文说。

> I got it. Thank you for your thorough analysis.

这句很自然，语气也符合“经理想赶快收住”的场景。更贴近原对话可以这样说：

```text
Okay, I get it. Thank you for your very thorough analysis.
```

小点：
- `I got it` 可以，但这里经理是当下回应，用 `I get it` 更像“好了，我明白了”。
- `very thorough analysis` 带一点客气但尴尬的味道，很适合这个场景。

这一轮先收住。刚才你已经能自己说出来这些核心块：
- `go over the profit and loss statement`
- `expenses are through the roof`
- `off the charts`
- `expenditures on... are out of control`
- `I'll look into it`
- `I'll take care of it`
- `thorough analysis`

现在来一个小整合：切换成第一人称复述，不要继续对话。请用 `I` 讲述刚才发生的事，4-5 句。

场景：我在一次公司会议里讨论 profit and loss statement（损益表）。Jill 指出公司经常性费用非常高，尤其是 entertainment（招待/娱乐）和 travel（差旅）。她举了几个账单例子：hotel charges（酒店费用）、spa treatments（水疗护理），还有 Wild Things 这个地方的一笔费用。我一开始说会调查，后来因为有点尴尬，就说会处理这件事。请用英文复述。

> I discussed profit and loss statement in a meeting of my company. Jill pointed out that our expenses were through the roof, especially the expenses on entertainment and travel. She took several bills for examples: hotel charges, spa treatments and expenditures at a place called Wild Things. At first, I said I would look into it, then it was a little embarrassing, so I said I would take care of it.

这个复述已经把故事顺序讲清楚了。这里主要修自然表达和几个老问题：具体报表前的 `the`，以及两个完整句子不要只用逗号连。

自然版本：

```text
I discussed the profit and loss statement at a company meeting. Jill pointed out that our expenses were through the roof, especially our expenditures on entertainment and travel. She gave several bills as examples: hotel charges, spa treatments, and an expense at a place called Wild Things. At first, I said I would look into it. Then it got a little embarrassing, so I said I would take care of it.
```

这里主要改四处：
- `the profit and loss statement`：这是一份具体的损益表，要加 `the`。
- `at a company meeting` 比 `in a meeting of my company` 自然。
- `gave several bills as examples`，不要说 `took ... for examples`。
- `I said I would look into it. Then...` 两个完整句子分开；你之前的逗号连接问题这里又露了一点点。

先不照抄上面的段落。换个很近的场景，练两个重点：

你在一次预算会议上讨论 training budget（培训预算）。财务经理说：培训费用太高了，并举了几张账单作为例子。你先说会调查，后来决定自己处理。请用英文说 3 句。

> I was discussing the training budget at a budget meeting. The financial manager said that the training expenses were through the roof, and she gave several bills as examples. I said I would look into it. Then I decided I would take care of it.

这次修得很干净：`the training budget`、`the training expenses` 都加对了，句子连接也稳了。

可以再压成更像口头汇报的版本：

```text
I was discussing the training budget at a budget meeting. The finance manager said the training expenses were through the roof and gave several bills as examples. I said I would look into it, but then I decided to take care of it myself.
```

小点：
- `finance manager` 更像职位名称；`financial manager` 也能懂，但不如它常见。
- `decided to take care of it myself` 更清楚地表达“我决定亲自处理”。

这一轮你已经把两个旧问题也顺手修好了：
- 具体预算/费用前的 `the`
- 两个完整句子之间用句号或 `but/and/so` 连接

现在做一个真实迁移。你想把这节课的表达用在哪个真实场景？比如：项目预算超支、团队差旅费太高、学习/健身花费失控、或者你自己公司的某类开支太高。你选一个场景，我来跟你对话。

> 学习/健身花费失控

好，这个场景很适合把商务表达迁移到生活里。现在换回直接对话，不要复述。你在和朋友聊自己的 spending（花销）。我演朋友。

Friend: You look worried. What’s going on?

你想说：我刚仔细看了一下这个月的支出，发现学习和健身上的花费高得离谱。请用英文直接回答朋友。

> I checked my expenses of this month, and found out that my expenses on study and workout were through the roof.

你把核心意思说出来了，`were through the roof` 用得很好。这里主要让它更像自然口语：

```text
I went over my expenses for this month and found that my spending on studying and working out was through the roof.
```

这里主要改四处：
- `went over` 比 `checked` 更贴这课，表示“仔细看/审阅”。
- `expenses for this month` 比 `expenses of this month` 自然。
- 生活场景里说 `spending on...` 比重复 `expenses on...` 更顺。
- `studying and working out` 比 `study and workout` 更自然；`workout` 作名词常是“一次训练”，这里动作更适合 `working out`。

先不照抄上面的句子。换个很近的场景：你仔细看了这个月的 app subscriptions（应用订阅）和 online courses（网课）支出，发现这些花销高得离谱。请用英文说。

> I went over my expenses on app subscriptions and online courses for this month and found that they were through the roof.

这句已经自然很多，`went over` 和 `through the roof` 都用上了。

可以稍微调一下顺序：

```text
I went over my expenses for this month on app subscriptions and online courses and found that they were through the roof.
```

或者更口语：

```text
I went over my spending on app subscriptions and online courses this month and found that it was through the roof.
```

小点：
- `spending` 当整体花销看，所以后面用 `it was`。
- 如果用 `expenses` 复数，`they were` 可以。

Friend: Wow. What exactly are you spending money on?

你想说：课程、健身房会员和补剂 supplement（补剂）上的支出都已经失控了。请用英文说。

> my expenses on lessons, gym membership and supplement are out of control.

这句意思很清楚，`out of control` 用对了。自然版本：

```text
My spending on lessons, my gym membership, and supplements is out of control.
```

小点：
- `spending on...` 在生活花销里很自然。
- `supplements` 要用复数，因为通常不是一种补剂。
- `spending` 是整体花销，所以后面用 `is`；如果说 `expenses`，可以用 `are`。

Friend: That sounds expensive. Are you going to do anything about it?

你想说：我会先调查一下哪些订阅最贵，然后这个周末处理。请用英文直接回答朋友。

> I'll look into which subscriptions are the most expensive, then take care of it this weekend.

这句很顺，两个关键表达都真的迁移出来了：

```text
I'll look into which subscriptions are the most expensive, then take care of it this weekend.
```

如果想更完整一点，可以补主语：

```text
I'll look into which subscriptions are the most expensive, and then I'll take care of it this weekend.
```

小点：这里 `look into` 是“调查/查清楚”；`take care of it` 是“采取行动解决”，你区分得对。

1. 你在公司会议里，想说：我们仔细看一下这些数字，确认没有错误。请用英文说。

> let's go over these numbers and make sure there's no mistake.

这句可以直接用，核心块 `go over these numbers` 很稳。小修一下单复数：

```text
Let's go over these numbers and make sure there are no mistakes.
```

小点：
- `these numbers` 是一组数字，可能不止一个错误，所以常说 `there are no mistakes`。
- 开头写作时 `Let's` 大写；口语不影响。

  例：`She gave several bills as examples.`
